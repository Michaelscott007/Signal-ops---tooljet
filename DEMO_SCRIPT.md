# SignalOps — 90-second demo

## 0–15 sec — Problem
Product teams receive evidence from GitHub, customers, support and internal teams. The hard part is not collecting it. The hard part is turning inconsistent evidence into a decision without losing provenance.

## 15–35 sec — Signal Inbox
Open the Signal Inbox. Show that public ToolJet GitHub issues are loaded read-only and appear alongside clearly labeled synthetic customer feedback. Filter by source, severity or review state.

## 35–60 sec — Review Workbench
Open one signal. The left side preserves the original evidence. The right side structures the interpretation: product area, severity, impact, recommended action and notes. Change one field to show that a PM can override the interpretation.

## 60–75 sec — Human decision
Approve, reject or send the signal for further discovery. The final decision is stored locally for the demo. The source evidence remains unchanged.

## 75–90 sec — Product takeaway
Return to Overview. Explain that the prioritization score is transparent decision support, not an AI roadmap oracle. The core product principle is simple: AI may structure evidence; consequential product judgment remains human-controlled.
