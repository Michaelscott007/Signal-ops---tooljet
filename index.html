Product brief
Why SignalOps exists
A small experiment in product evidence, human judgment and AI boundaries.

      


      

        
Problem

        
Product teams receive evidence from GitHub issues, customer conversations, support, feature requests and internal observations. Those signals arrive in inconsistent formats and are difficult to compare.


        
User

        
A PM working across an enterprise application platform who needs to understand what deserves attention without losing source context.


        
Product principles

        
    
          
  • Preserve original evidence.
    • 
          
  • Make uncertainty visible.
    • 
          
  • Use AI to structure, not silently decide.
    • 
          
  • Keep prioritization transparent and challengeable.
    • 
          
  • Never fabricate customer truth.
    • 
        


        
What AI could do next

        
Summarize long reports, suggest product areas, cluster similar issues and flag missing context. It should not invent prevalence, revenue impact or final roadmap priority.


        
What I would validate next

        
Compare AI-assisted triage against PM-labeled examples and measure whether review time falls without reducing classification quality or decision confidence.
